Returns, Exchange & Delivery Policy

Khyber Traders · Wholesale Veterinary Pharmacy, Karachi

Order & Delivery Terms

  • Delivery charges depend on your area and vehicle — confirmed at time of order
  • Orders are dispatched after payment confirmation
  • Delivery time will be confirmed by our WhatsApp representative
  • Cold chain items (vaccines) are only delivered within Karachi, due to cold storage requirements

Returns & Exchange Policy

  • Unopened items may be returned within 7 days of delivery — delivery charges apply
  • Opened items are not eligible for return or exchange
  • To ensure reliable, authentic & storage-maintained veterinary supplies, the following are not eligible for return or exchange under any condition:
    1. Vaccines
    2. Sprays
    3. Imported products
    4. Surgical items
    5. Items with less than 6 months to expiry
    6. Any item with damaged or soiled packaging

Questions about an order, return, or delivery? Message us directly on WhatsApp and our representative will help.
